Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 September 2026

This site is a personal portfolio belonging to an individual who goes by Chez. It is a static website: there are no accounts, no login, no contact form and no comment section. This page describes exactly what happens to data when you visit, and nothing more.

What this site collects

Nothing directly. The site sets no cookies of its own, runs no analytics, no advertising scripts and no tracking pixels, and has no database. There is no field anywhere on the site for you to enter personal information, so none is gathered.

What third parties may see

A few things happen automatically when any website loads, and they are worth stating plainly:

  • Hosting. This site is served by Render. Like nearly all web hosts, Render may record standard server request information such as your IP address, the time of the request, the page requested and your browser's user agent. This is handled by Render under its own privacy policy, not by me, and I do not build reports from it.
  • Fonts. The typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. Requesting a font means your browser contacts Google's servers, which makes your IP address visible to Google. That is governed by Google's own privacy policy. If you would rather this did not happen, the fonts can be self hosted instead.
  • Videos. Any clips shown are files served from this same site. They are not embedded from YouTube, Vimeo or any other video platform, so no third party video tracking is involved.

Links away from this site

The contact section links to a Discord profile. Once you follow that link you are on Discord, and Discord's own privacy policy and terms apply to whatever happens there. I have no control over how other services handle your data.

Messages you send me

If you contact me on Discord, I will have whatever you choose to send: your Discord username and the contents of your messages. I use that only to discuss and carry out the work being talked about. I do not sell it, publish it or pass it to anyone else. Project details shared with me while discussing work are treated as confidential.

Children

This site is aimed at people commissioning development work. It does not knowingly gather information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because the site stores no personal data, there is nothing held here to access, correct or erase. If you have messaged me on Discord and want that conversation deleted, ask and I will delete my side of it.

Changes

If the site gains anything that changes the picture, such as analytics or a contact form, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change with it.
